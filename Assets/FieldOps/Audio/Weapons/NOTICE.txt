Weapon sound effects (processed derivatives of third-party recordings)

SHOTS - pistol_shot.wav, rifle_shot.wav, smg_shot.wav, shotgun_shot.wav, sniper_shot.wav
  Source: "Gunshot Sounds" (cz.wav, sks.wav, mosin.wav, shotty.wav), uploaded to OpenGameArt by Tabasco
          https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
  Author: Vincent Sevedge ("Copyright (c) 2009 Vincent Sevedge", as stated in the downloaded archive's creativecommons.txt)
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0) - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Note: the OpenGameArt page labels the pack CC0, but the archive itself says CC BY 3.0. The stricter CC BY 3.0 terms are followed here (attribution given).
  Changes made (adaptation): each file is a single shot cut from a longer recording, converted to mono 44.1 kHz, high-pass filtered, faded and peak-normalized.
          smg_shot.wav is the rifle (sks) shot raised in pitch (x1.35), high-passed and shortened.

RELOADS - pistol_reload.wav, rifle_reload.wav, smg_reload.wav, sniper_reload.wav, shotgun_reload.wav
  Source: "Gun reload sounds" by Joaken - https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
  License: CC0 (credit not required, given as a courtesy)
  Changes made: converted to mono, faded and peak-normalized. smg_reload.wav is the rifle reload raised in pitch (x1.12).
          shotgun_reload.wav is three generated shell-insertion ticks followed by the pump-cock recording.
  sniper_reload.wav is NOT from this pack: it is the bolt-working sound cut from the "Gunshot Sounds" Mosin recording above (CC BY 3.0, Vincent Sevedge), high-passed, with silence added.

CYCLE SOUNDS - sniper_bolt.wav, shotgun_pump.wav
  Cut from the files above (no new recordings): sniper_bolt.wav is the 0.30 to 1.12 s stretch of sniper_reload.wav (the bolt being worked, from the Mosin recording by Vincent Sevedge, CC BY 3.0);
  shotgun_pump.wav is the 1.50 to 1.90 s stretch of shotgun_reload.wav (the pump, from the "Gun reload sounds" recording by Joaken, CC0).
  Changes made: trimmed with 10 ms fades. They play after each shot of those two weapons, during the fire cooldown.

The target-hit and empty-magazine click sounds are generated by code (see SoundSynth.cs).
Files were processed once with an offline script; the original long recordings are not included in this repository.
